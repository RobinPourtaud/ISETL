# Attribution and license scope

Original ISETL Web contributions are [MIT licensed](LICENSE). That grant does not relicense the historical interpreter, manuals, libraries, executables, or the compiled interpreter derived from them.

## Original interpreter and manuals

The interpreter’s `ISETLSRC/REV_INFO.C` identifies Gary Levin as its author, developed at Clarkson University, copyright 1986–1990.

The “ISETL Copyright Information” section in [`ISETL-DOC/ISETL1.txt`](ISETL-DOC/ISETL1.txt) and [`ISETL-DOC/ISETL3.txt`](ISETL-DOC/ISETL3.txt) states:

> This manual and the accompanying software may be freely copied, subject to
> the restriction that it not be sold for profit. (This would permit bulk
> copying and sale at cost.) The software is offered as-is, but we will
> attempt to correct errors in our code.

The original notice is reproduced in [LICENSE-LEGACY.txt](LICENSE-LEGACY.txt). This is a historical copying permission with a non-profit-sale restriction, not the MIT license. The repository as a whole should not be described as uniformly MIT licensed.

## Other historical contributions

Plotting libraries retain their original author comments, including Don Muench’s modifications in `ISETLW/DONPLOT.STL`.

`ISETLSRC/ILE.C` contains the Evans & Sutherland line editor copyright and permission notice, also reproduced in the manuals and `LICENSE-LEGACY.txt`. That file remains unchanged; the browser uses CodeMirror and does not compile the line editor.

Third-party JavaScript packages and the Emscripten runtime retain their respective licenses.
